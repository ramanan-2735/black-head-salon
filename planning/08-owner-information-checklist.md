# 08 — Owner Information Checklist

> Black Head Salon — Phase 1 planning deliverable. Source: approved planning report (2026-10-18). Tags: P0/P1/P2 priority · [REAL] [PROTO] [3RD] [OWNER].

## 19. Salon owner information checklist (→ `08-owner-information-checklist.md`)

Business: legal/trading name & spelling · logo files (SVG/PNG) & any brand colours · address + Google Maps pin + Google Business Profile link · phone, WhatsApp number, email · Instagram/other socials · opening hours per day incl. breaks · holidays/closure policy.
Services: full list with categories, descriptions, prices (fixed or "from"), durations, buffer/cleanup time · popular services · packages/combos · current offers & validity · kids' services?
Booking: confirmation mode · minimum notice · how far ahead · cancellation/reschedule policy · no-show policy · walk-in policy · who manages bookings day to day.
Staff: barber names, photos, short bios, services each performs, weekly schedules, consent to appear online.
Imagery: salon interior/exterior photos · haircut photos + **customer consent to publish** · permission for before/after · photographer session?
Content: brand story · years in business / facts **they can verify** · review sources (Google profile link).
Tech: preferred notification channels · domain (existing?) · email domain · who owns hosting accounts · admin users' emails · budget for recurring services · privacy contact person.
